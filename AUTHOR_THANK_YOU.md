# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to John Cooper, Ilias Diakonikolas, Mingchen Ma, and Frederic Sala
for presenting a clear hybrid sequence-model setting with explicit synthetic
tasks, theorem anchors, constructive mechanisms, and parameterized
comparisons. The paper’s structure made it possible to separate the
recurrent-plus-local-attention mechanism from stronger wording that did not
survive the scoped audit.

This repository is an independent reproduction and audit. It does not imply
review, approval, endorsement, or collaboration by the paper’s authors.
